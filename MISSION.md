# Mission — Tuner

Tuner is, and will remain, free — on the web, always.

The web is the home platform. [tuner.obfusco.us](https://tuner.obfusco.us) is free to open, free of ads, and free of distraction, with no account, no paywall, and no expiration. That's the one guarantee.

The app stores are an extension of that reach, not a replacement for it. Tuner shows up on Android and iOS so it can sit on a home screen and open full-screen without a browser chrome around it — but those are platforms Tuner doesn't control. Store policies, review processes, developer account costs, and OS changes are outside this project's hands, so Tuner can't promise the same permanence there that it promises on the free web. If an app store version is ever discontinued, delisted, or paid to maintain, the web version keeps going regardless.

Free and public, on the ground truth of the open web — everything else is a best effort on top of that.
